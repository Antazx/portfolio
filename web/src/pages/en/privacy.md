## 2. Data and purposes

**Contact enquiries.** Once the form is operational, it may process your name, email address, message, and selected language so the enquiry can be read and answered. The function may also receive technical data, including an IP address, for abuse controls. Enquiries will not be added to a marketing list.

The form is not currently working in production. In the meantime, you can email the address above; if you do, the message and any personal data it contains will be processed to reply.

**Newsletter.** The newsletter is inactive. If enabled, it would process your email address, selected language, signup request, subscription confirmation, and information needed to record and honour an unsubscribe. These data would be used to send portfolio updates in the selected language. No campaigns will be sent while the service remains disabled.

**Operation and security.** Hosting may generate technical data needed to deliver and protect the site, such as the date, requested path, request result, or IP address. Exact logs depend on the provider’s actual configuration and remain to be verified.

**Theme preference.** The site stores the browser’s light or dark theme preference in local storage. The code does not use it to identify you or build profiles. The lawyer will confirm whether further information or measures are required.

No analytics or advertising tracking has been enabled, according to the owner.
